Slipstick window, three directions

Each one is drawn at actual size, docked bottom-right above the taskbar. Pick a state to see how all three handle it. Click an answer to copy it, and click Steps to open the working.

State
Behind the window

A. Datasheet

Light, like a printed datasheet. Condensed numerals, answer on the left, and a flag shows as a highlighted strip.

B. Enamel

Coloured like a pedal enclosure. Answer on the right like a calculator display, and a lamp lights when there is something to read.

C. Graphite

Dark and neutral, closest to Windows itself. Adds one small line above the answer showing the formula it used.
